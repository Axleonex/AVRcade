VRClient supports a specific, curated set of games and builds. **The platform does not
promise, guarantee, or imply that it works with every game, every build, every storefront,
every hardware configuration, or any particular title.** Compatibility is limited to builds
the platform can positively identify as supported, and even then is provided on an "as is"
and "as available" basis without warranty of any kind. Support for a title or build may be
added, changed, or removed at any time. A game working today is not a commitment that it
will work tomorrow, or that any other game will work at all.

Using VRClient is the user's choice and the user's responsibility. By using the platform the
user is responsible for:

- ensuring they own a legitimate, properly licensed copy of any game they convert;

- accepting the risks of running third-party software against their games, including the

The platform is a tool. It does not, and cannot, grant the user any rights they do not
otherwise have in the underlying game.

## 3. No anti-cheat circumvention

VRClient is designed for single-player and offline play of supported builds. **The platform
does not circumvent, disable, defeat, evade, tamper with, or interfere with any anti-cheat,
anti-tamper, or digital-rights-management technology.** Where it detects such technology, it
does so only to **refuse** to operate against that target. The platform must not be used to
gain any advantage in online or competitive play, and it provides no capability to do so.

Users are reminded that running third-party software against online or anti-cheat-protected
games may violate those games' terms and may result in penalties imposed by the publisher,
up to and including account suspension or ban. The platform's default behavior is to refuse
such targets specifically to reduce this risk, but the user remains responsible for their
own conduct and account standing.

## 4. Modding support is not permission

Some games officially support modding. Official or community modding support may make a game
a better candidate for conversion, but **it is not a grant of permission to inject into
online sessions, anti-cheat-protected modes, or unsupported builds.** The platform evaluates
identity, anti-cheat, online-mode, source, and launch-mode conditions independently of
modding posture, and the presence of mod support does not relax any of those checks.
