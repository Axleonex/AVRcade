AVRcade - before you install

AVRcade is a launcher for playing flat PC games in VR. For each supported game
it offers four ways to play: VR only, VR + mods, Mods without VR, and Vanilla.

Supported games in this release:
* R.E.P.O., Lethal Company, PEAK, Content Warning and Big Walk, through
  community Unity VR mods that AVRcade downloads and verifies for you.
* RV There Yet?, through UEVR and a community profile (experimental).
* Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas (classic), through AVRcade's own VR bridge
  (experimental).
* Cyberpunk 2077: monitor modes work out of the box. "Install VR backend"
  adds the VR plugins and downloads the modding frameworks they need
  (experimental; one specific game build).

You provide:
* The game, owned and installed through Steam.
* A PC VR headset connection through SteamVR or Virtual Desktop.
* Optionally a mod manager (r2modman, Thunderstore Mod Manager, Vortex, or the
  one you already use) for the two modded modes.

AVRcade bundles its own .NET runtime; nothing else needs installing. The
installer is per-user and does not ask for administrator access.

Safety: VR conversions are mods. Use modded modes only in private sessions
with friends who agreed to it. AVRcade asks you to confirm before a modded
launch and refuses games with anti-cheat.

This build is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to
confirm that you want to run it.
